Proposed decision rules

  1. Use 2–4 valid readings from distinct methods on the same finger.
  2. Different US sizes or a mixture of below-chart and in-chart readings → remeasure. All below chart → size unavailable. A competitor size above US 12 blocks confirmation.
  3. A strict OK majority recommends a size: 2 of 2, 2 of 3, or 3 of 4.
  4. Without an OK majority, mixed Loose and Tight readings → remeasure.
  5. Otherwise, an Tight majority advances to the next finger; a Loose majority also advances to the next finger.
  6. Ties → remeasure. After the third finger, choose three new fingers.
These are proposed product rules, implemented in the Explore flow. Agreement among readings is not a guarantee of physical fit.
