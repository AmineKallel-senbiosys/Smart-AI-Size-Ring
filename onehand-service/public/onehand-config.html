One-hand settings

These values are used only by /onehand (one photo of the back of the hand). The dashboard tables and the normal measurement are not affected. A change applies to the next measurement straight away. Male and female can have different values: pick the tab, edit, then Save (both tabs are saved together).

Offsets (mm) edited

C = π × W − offset, one value per finger.

Size limits (mm)

A finger below the US 6 limit is US 6, between US 6 and US 8 is US 8, and so on. Above the US 12 limit there is no size.

Borderline distance (mm)

A finger closer than this to the top of its size also shows the next larger size (never a smaller one). 0 turns it off.
