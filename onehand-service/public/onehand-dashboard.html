One-hand accuracy

Size only, no fit message. Each finger is the back photo alone: C = π × W − offset, then the size bands. A finger counts as correct when the size the person confirmed with the ring kit is the size the app showed first, or the neighbour shown as "or". Each test is calculated with the values of its own gender, as set on /onehand/config (male and female can differ).

Find the best values

Searches the 5 offsets and the 4 limits that give the most fingers in the right size band, starting from the values active on /onehand (the borderline rule is left out of the search and scanned afterwards). Then it checks itself: it trains on 4/5 of the people and tests on the 1/5 it has not seen, five times. If that held-out score is clearly lower than the fitted one, the values are fitted to the sample and will not hold on new people.
